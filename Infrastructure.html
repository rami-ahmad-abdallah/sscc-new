Strategic partner

Since the establishment of Scientific Systems Company, a business model has been developed based on continuous improvement and the enhancement of operational capabilities in the infrastructure sector, relying on accumulated practical experience and a proven execution record in project management and delivery in accordance with best technical and administrative practices. This approach has enabled the company to strengthen its market presence and successfully deliver high-quality, value-driven projects.
As part of its transition to a more mature phase in terms of corporate governance and operational stability, the company has adopted a structured expansion strategy focused on building sustainable institutional partnerships. Accordingly, a strategic partnership based on institutional ownership was established with Al-Buniah Al-Asasiah Contracting Company, aiming to achieve effective integration in regulatory accreditations, execution capabilities, and compliance requirements related to government projects.
This institutional integration has enhanced Scientific Systems Company’s readiness for qualified participation in government works, enabled direct engagement with relevant authorities, and ensured project execution in accordance with approved regulatory frameworks and technical and procedural standards adopted by competent government entities, thereby supporting sustainable growth and reinforcing institutional trust among partners and clients.

Project Management

We manage projects from the initial planning phase through to final delivery, applying the necessary knowledge, expertise, and modern technologies to ensure timely execution within the available budget and in full compliance with specified standards and requirements.

Our Project Management Skills

We excel in problem analysis, identifying root causes, and developing practical, actionable solutions. For example, when technical data is inaccurate and may result in project delays, the project manager is responsible for determining the source of the inaccuracy, resolving the issue, and implementing corrective measures to maintain workflow efficiency and ensure on-schedule delivery.

Structuring and coordinating the various project activities and operations, including preparing timelines and defining project costs within an approved budget, to ensure project delivery according to the planned schedule and with maximum efficiency.

Overseeing and monitoring all project activities to maintain operational organization and ensure the project remains on the right course, supporting disciplined execution and adherence to the planned workflow.

Developing a clear and comprehensive project timeline that includes all activities, while defining expected costs within the allocated budget, and continuously enhancing performance through improved planning processes and optimized project management practices.
