WHO WE ARE ?

Scientific Systems Construction Company was established with the aim of providing creative solutions in the urban landscape as an extension of the great growth and prosperity witnessed by the Kingdom in line with the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030.

OUR VISION

To be one of the leading companies in the field of contracting and to rise to become the most trusted and first service provider in the Kingdom and in the Middle East.

OUR MESSAGE

Providing exceptional services to our customers in accordance with international quality standards, keeping pace with technological development and professional commitment to honesty, honesty and accuracy, which will help us to be one of the leading companies in providing construction services for huge projects in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the Middle East.

OUR CHAIRMAN SPEECH

The pursuit of success is a never-ending journey, and one of the secrets of this success is perseverance in pursuing the goal. Because the contracting sector is considered one of the most important sectors on which the state’s economy is based, and in keeping with our rational government, its texts, and Vision 2030, our goal was to define a clear vision for the future and develop the present in a way that gives an impression of the urban renaissance of our kingdom to the world. We work according to a precise pattern and according to a system of fixed laws that always puts us at the top.

Networking Works

Scientific Systems Construction Company employs robust networking strategies that extend far beyond information technology, creating a dynamic ecosystem essential for modern construction project delivery. This encompasses intricate professional relationship networks—connecting key personnel, subcontractors, suppliers, and regulatory bodies—which are crucial for resource acquisition and project approvals. Furthermore, the company relies heavily on sophisticated physical logistical networks: coordinating complex supply chains for materials delivery, managing heavy equipment movement across various job sites, and establishing efficient onsite communication loops among diverse trade teams to ensure seamless project execution and timely completion.
