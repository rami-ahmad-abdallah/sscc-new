ABOUT SSCC

Who we are

Scientific Systems Contracting Company was established with the purpose of providing innovative solutions that enrich the urban landscape, in alignment with the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030 and its ongoing journey of growth and prosperity. Guided by our forward-looking vision, the company has successfully built a distinguished reputation as a leading model in the contracting sector, achieving notable accomplishments across various fields including construction, infrastructure, interior and exterior design, and project management.

OUR VISION

To be one of the leading companies in the contracting sector, advancing to become the most trusted service provider and the first choice in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and across the Middle East.

OUR VALUES

To deliver exceptional services to our clients in accordance with the highest international quality standards, while embracing technological advancement and upholding the highest levels of professionalism, integrity, honesty, and precision. Through these principles, we aim to become a leading provider of construction and development services for major projects in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the Middle East.

Business Plan

SSCC’s business plan focuses on precise planning and professional execution of infrastructure projects, ensuring quality delivery, schedule compliance, and cost efficiency to maximize client value

Capability & Capacity

SSCC has the technical capability and operational capacity to deliver infrastructure projects efficiently, supported by qualified engineering teams, specialized equipment, and integrated project management systems that ensure quality, safety, and on-time delivery.
